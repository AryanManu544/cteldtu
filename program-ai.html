Advanced Program in Artificial Intelligence

Master AI fundamentals and advanced techniques

Service Provider: DTU Centre for Executive Programmes

Features

Application Deadline: 15th March 2026 Duration: 6 Months Mode: Online

Program Objectives

  • The Certificate Program in Artificial Intelligence is a comprehensive course that provides participants with a foundational understanding of artificial intelligence, AI tools, algorithms, and their industrial applications.
  • The course will equip participants with the knowledge and practical skills necessary to proficiently apply machine learning techniques to tackle complex problems across diverse domains such as sales and marketing, medical diagnostics, to sports analytics.
  • The course is thoughtfully crafted with a fine balance between theory and practice, with a special focus on learners from non-CS backgrounds.
  • Contemporary industry case studies and practice sessions have been curated to provide hands-on experience of applying advanced machine learning techniques, that are used by leading tech and non-tech companies (e.g., Google, Amazon, Coca-Cola, Flipkart, think tanks, Civil and Mechanical Engineering firms, Power companies, Government agencies, etc.).

Evaluation Methodology

Participants will be evaluated through weekly assignments (40%), mid-term project (20%), and capstone project (40%). Active participation in live sessions and peer discussions will also be considered.

Assignments/Case-Projects/etc.

  • • Weekly coding assignments on machine learning algorithms
  • • Mid-term project: Build a complete ML pipeline for a real dataset
  • • Case study analyses of industry AI implementations
  • • Final capstone project with industry problem statement

Pedagogy

The program follows a blended learning approach combining live online sessions, self-paced learning, hands-on projects, and peer collaboration. Each module includes theory sessions, practical demonstrations, coding exercises, and real-world case studies.

Program Content

Comprehensive curriculum covering AI fundamentals, machine learning, deep learning, NLP, computer vision, and production deployment. Includes 60 hours of live instruction, 40 hours of assignments, 20-hour capstone project, and additional resources for self-study.

Selection Criteria

Applicants will be selected based on educational background, work experience, and a brief statement of purpose. Preference will be given to candidates with programming experience and strong quantitative skills.

Admission Criteria

• Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, or related quantitative field. Basic programming knowledge (Python preferred).
• Minimum 50% marks in graduation.
• Working professionals preferred but fresh graduates may also apply.

Who should attend / Target Segment?

  • Graduates or Post-Graduates in Engineering, Technology, Computer Science, IT, Mathematical Sciences and related disciplines will be preferred.
  • Preference to working professionals with minimum 1 years of prior experience in IT, software, technology, engineering or related domains.
